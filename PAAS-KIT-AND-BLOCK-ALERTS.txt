1. paas-kit (eb15645)  -  rtk + Headroom for every user; the coding plugins in the paas Market
     sh paas-kit/install.sh
       (model address NOT FOUND:  sh paas-kit/install.sh --upstream https://<your GLM address>)
     paas-kit status
   New in this version: its guard no longer refuses a command just because a file it writes or a commit message
   mentions the tool, and it now also catches calls hidden in "su -c" and "eval". Nothing else changed since 7b0c5df.
   Already on 7b0c5df? Running install.sh again updates it; settings in /etc/paas-kit/kit.json are kept.
