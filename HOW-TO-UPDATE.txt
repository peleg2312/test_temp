New in 993555f: the chat's memory, readable (web UI)
  - The button next to the prompt now reads "Memory 34%" with a small ring that fills up: green, amber from 50%, red
    from 80% ("empty" in a new chat). Hover it: it says what that means and what to do.
  - Click it: "How full is this chat?" - a bar, one sentence of advice, what fills it (what Claude read / what it wrote,
    in pages of text, the token numbers small beside them) and what to do when it fills up (/compact, a new chat, ask for
    summaries instead of whole logs).
  - No more "0 tokens until I refresh": the GLM model reports its usage only at the end of a request, so the page got a
    zero first and never the real number. Zero readings are now ignored, and at the end of every answer the page reads
    the real number by itself (what the refresh did).
  - Comes with the new web UI bundle (in this zip, 1.37.5-paas, a new build): the installer run in step 2 unpacks it,
    then restart the UI (the systemctl line in step 2). Everyone: reload the browser page once after the restart.

        https://; the token is still required, but nothing is encrypted on the network (the docs token and the teams'
        notes could be read by someone capturing traffic). The sign-in page stays HTTPS. --https goes back.
